# Slice 6 — Photos ✅

_Moved from `PLAN.md` on 2026-10-24, as it stood._

Wallie's choices (2026-10-11): one optional photo on a **sighting** or a **feeding where King was seen** (a left-out feeding takes none); a **shared photo** posted from `/photos` with no entry. Uploads go **through the server**, in the `/api/king` writes group (US-only, write limit, `CanPost`), and photos are **served by the server**, which serves nothing hidden. A photo is **public at once** and can be hidden by an admin: on its own (the entry stays), with its entry, or with its poster — **hidden and restorable, never deleted by an admin** (a photo reported to NCMEC must be kept a year, 18 U.S.C. § 2258A(h); an admin delete could destroy evidence). Shown on **Home** (thumbnail in Lately; the newest visible photo, of any kind, is the crowned avatar — no framing yet, the current `object-position` applies), **History** (thumbnail on the entry's line), and **`/photos`** (every visible photo newest first, 50 at a time with Show older, "Share a photo"; linked under Lately, from History and in the footer). Alt text tells how the photo was added, like its history line ("Photo from a sighting by Sarah near Porch, Oct 8 at 4:12 PM"; "Photo shared by Sarah on Oct 8"); no captions. A shared photo is never a sighting: it's on `/photos` only — never in Lately, History, heat or the status chips — because a camera-roll photo may be months old.

Checked (2026-10-11): East US Hot LRS $0.0208/GB-month, writes $0.05 / reads $0.004 per 10K (Azure Retail Prices API) — ~0.8 GB a year at 5 photos a day, cents a month; `Aspire.Hosting.Azure.Storage` and `Aspire.Azure.Storage.Blobs` 13.6.1 exist (Azurite emulator locally; the client brings `Azure.Storage.Blobs` + `Azure.Identity` — **new dependencies**). Not yet checked: Azurite under the e2e AppHost on Wallie's Mac (step 1 starts there).

**Azure changes — approved by Wallie (2026-10-11)** (added to `provision.sh`, dry-run with a stub `az` before Wallie runs it):
1. Storage account **`kingofweesphotos`** (new, separate from backups: soft delete is per account, and the backup account's 30-day delete rule must never touch photos): StorageV2, Standard_LRS, Hot, TLS 1.2, shared keys off, public blob access off; container `photos` (private); **blob soft delete 30 days** (recovers an undo or a mistake; the nightly dump doesn't include photos). Cost: storage above; the account itself is free.
2. **System-assigned managed identity** on the app `kingofwees` (it has none today), carried in the app definition so reruns keep it.
3. Role **Storage Blob Data Contributor** for that identity, scoped to the `photos` container only.
4. App setting (not a secret): the blob service address `https://kingofweesphotos.blob.core.windows.net` as the Aspire `photos` connection.
