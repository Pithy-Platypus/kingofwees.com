# Small gaps from the Failures investigation ✅

_Moved from `PLAN.md` on 2026-10-24, as it stood._

Found in the 2026-10-19 Failures investigation (Telemetry section). Browsers asked for `/favicon.ico` (367 in 30 days), `/apple-touch-icon*.png` (221) and crawlers for `/robots.txt` (53): all 404, the site has none. And 46 of the 56 499s were thumbnails a visitor gave up on: blob downloads in a revision's first 3 minutes have a p95 of 4.2 s against 0.35 s later, 47 of 53 cancelled downloads fall there, and the managed-identity token (`GET /msi/token`) takes 1.9–3.2 s on first use. Mongo is warm before traffic (`/health` readiness checks it); photo storage deliberately isn't in `/health`.

Decided (Wallie, 2026-10-20): the crown (`CrownIcon`) is the site icon; the site stays out of search engines through `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on every response, with a `robots.txt` that allows crawling except `/api/` (a crawler blocked by `Disallow: /` never sees the noindex, and Google may still list a bare linked address).
